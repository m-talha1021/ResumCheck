Learn why ATS-friendly resumes are essential for landing interviews and how ResumeIQ helps you optimize your resume.

Understanding ATS

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers to collect, organize, and filter job applications. Before a recruiter reads your resume, the ATS scans it for keywords, skills, formatting, and job relevance.

If your resume isn't ATS-friendly, it may never reach a hiring manager—even if you're highly qualified for the position.
